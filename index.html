Wave Equation

🔑 Key Difference

y–t Graph One particle is selected. Its position x stays fixed while time t changes.
y–x Graph Time is fixed at one instant. The graph shows all particles at different positions along the wave.
Green Particle The green particle is the particle being followed in the y–t graph.

Physics Relationships

Angular frequency: ω = 2πf

Wave number: k = 2π/λ

Wave speed: v = fλ
