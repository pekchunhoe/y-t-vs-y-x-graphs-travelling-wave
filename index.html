6. Wave Equation

The simulation uses the travelling-wave equation:

y(x,t) = A sin(kx − ωt)

where:

  • A = amplitude
  • λ = wavelength
  • f = frequency
  • k = 2π/λ = wave number
  • ω = 2πf = angular frequency
  • v = fλ = wave speed

Remember: The wave travels horizontally, while individual particles oscillate vertically about their equilibrium positions.
